CONTRACT: Seoul Shadows (서울의 그림자) is a third-person stealth game set in a fictional night-time Seoul. You are a freelance operative: take contracts at your safehouse, find people and evidence without being spotted, get paid, upgrade, and work towards $1,000,000.

No one is harmed: guards warn, escort or report you, and failed contracts can be retried.

Features

  • Two districts to explore: Gangnam (glass towers, neon Hangul signage, food alley, subway) and Myeongdong (street-food carts, cosmetics shops, the red-brick cathedral).
  • Five contracts, three operatives with their own strengths, 13 upgrades and outfits.
  • A living crowd, guards with line of sight and suspicion, terminals to hack, people to escort.
  • GPS Life mode (Android): walk in the real world and your operative walks the city. Distance and steps earn XP, rewards, missions and collectibles. Only totals are kept: no location data is shown, stored or sent.
  • Original synthesized music and ambience.

Controls

PC: WASD move · mouse look · Shift sprint · Ctrl crouch · Space jump · E interact (hold for terminals) · M map · Esc pause. Gamepads are supported.
Android: on-screen joystick, drag to look, touch buttons.

Requirements

  • Windows 10/11 64-bit with DirectX 11, or Linux 64-bit with Vulkan / OpenGL 4.5. Runs at about 60 fps on Intel UHD integrated graphics with the Low preset (measured on Linux).
  • Android 8.0+ (64-bit). The GPS mode needs location and, for steps, "Physical activity".

Installing

  • itch app: Install, then Launch (updates automatically).
  • Windows: unzip and run Contract_SeoulShadows.exe. The game is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": click More info → Run anyway.
  • Linux: unzip, then chmod +x Contract_SeoulShadows.x86_64 and run it.
  • Android: download the APK on the phone, allow your browser to install unknown apps, and install. Play Protect may warn about an unknown developer: Install anyway.

Safety (GPS mode)

Watch your surroundings, not the screen, and never use the phone while crossing a road. You can pause the walking session at any time.

Status

In development. Built with Unity 6 (URP). All characters, companies and events are fictional. Korean font: Noto Sans KR (OFL). Textures: Poly Haven (CC0).
